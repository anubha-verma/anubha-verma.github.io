About me

These days, I am shifting gears between tech and automotive features as an editor at SlashGear.

Some eons ago, I was working at S. Chand’s Mylestone editing K‑8 print and digital materials. I also developed and helped maintain the in-house style guide to bring consistency across the whole team.

Then Deloitte came knocking, and later Johnson & Johnson. Suddenly, textbooks were out and I was knee-deep in training modules, e-learning content, PPTs, PDFs, Figma workflows, gamified courses, accessible content — you name it, and I have probably reviewed it! This is also where I learned to “listen” to a page the way a low-vision user would via a screen reader. Tools like JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver would read every unlabeled button and every vague “click here” out loud. Imagine hearing “button, button, button” with no idea what any of them do. Trust me, it’s not pretty. Turns out, a color that fails a contrast check is just as bad as a dangling modifier. I’ve caught (and fixed) more than my fair share of both.

Honestly, if it has words in it, I've probably edited something like it. Need a second pair of eyes? I'm just an email away.

Samsung Galaxy S26

The Galaxy S26 sits under the S26 Ultra with a smaller battery ,: 4,300 mAh to the Ultra's 5,000 mAh. Samsung still advertises it as having the same 31 hours, max, of battery life during video playback. How does that play out in testing? Once again, each source testing this gets the sources get differing results.

GSMArena's battery testing of the S26 shows it getting less battery life in every category compared to the Pixel 11 Pro, with one exception: gaming two exceptions: calls and gaming. The S26 gets 27 hours and 37 minutes in calls and 9 hours and 27 minutes there in gaming, while the Pixel 11 Pro manages 23 hours and 50 minutes and 9 hours and 1913 minutes, respectively.

Phone ArenaPhoneArena gives the S26 first place in every category but video playback; the Pixel 11 Pro manages 11 hours and 4 minutes to the Galaxy S26's 8 hours and 54 minutes. It also gives the Galaxy S26 better overall battery life at 6 hours and 37 minutes versus the Pixel 11 Pro's 5 hours and 44 minutes of battery life, although GSMArena disputes this;: in its testing, the Pixel 11 Pro endures 15 hours and 38 minutes overall compared to the S26's 15 hours and 20 minutes. Although the race is definitely much closer here, we'd like to remind readers that the S26 has a battery that's 500 mAh less than the Pixel 11 Pro. The race is much closer here, and it's worth remembering that the S26's battery is 550 mAh smaller than the Pixel 11 Pro's. Typically, bigger, premium pro versions of phones have better battery life, so it's impressive that a non-pro flagship can compete with — and sometimes beat — the Pixel 11 Pro.

What changed and why:
  • Fact-checked every figure against GSMArena's tests and the official Google Pixel and Samsung spec pages: added the calls category the draft missed, corrected the Pixel's gaming time (19 to 13 minutes) and fixed the battery gap (500 to 550 mAh).
  • Made names and numbers consistent: "Pixel 11" to "Pixel 11 Pro," "Phone Arena" to "PhoneArena," and "6 hours and 37 minutes" to match every other figure.
  • Fixed punctuation: a stray space and comma became a colon, and a colon now introduces GSMArena's counterpoint. Adding "with" lets the sentence read correctly around the em dashes.
  • Tightened the prose by cutting filler such as "definitely," "we'd like to remind readers" and a repeated "of battery life."
